# Indexed Drone Industry

Copyright 2026 Indexed Drone Industry contributors.

The index cites manufacturers, operators, standards bodies and regulators. Product names and marks identify the subjects of the records. Citations link to third-party publications under their original terms.

## Data and map attribution

- **Drone Authority.** [FAA Remote ID Declarations of Compliance (Snapshot)](https://droneauthority.org/data/faa-remote-id-declarations), September 26, 2026. [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Adapted by deduplicating declared makes and retaining selected model and FAA record links. The snapshot covers accepted declarations. A declared make may be a retail brand. The records establish neither procurement eligibility nor market share.
- **U.S. Geological Survey.** [Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 Data Release](https://www.usgs.gov/data/mineral-commodity-summaries-2026-data-release), DOI [10.5066/P1WKQ63T](https://doi.org/10.5066/P1WKQ63T). Marked [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Adapted from the commodity CSV to selected country-level mine production, reserves, copper refinery production, and alumina refinery production.
- **U.S. Geological Survey.** [Mineral facilities of Latin America and the Caribbean](https://doi.org/10.5066/F7MG7MM6). Public-domain U.S. government data. Selected records adapted from the 2014 facility inventory.
- **U.S. Geological Survey.** [Compilation of Geospatial Data (GIS) for the Mineral Industries and Related Infrastructure of Africa](https://doi.org/10.5066/P97EQWXP). Marked [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Selected facility records adapted from the 2021 data release.
- **U.S. Geological Survey.** [Production of mineral commodities and geospatial map of the mineral industries and related infrastructure of China](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/ofr20261018/full#ofr20261018-t05). Selected Table 5 facility records adapted from this U.S. government report. The index uses tabular records from the report.
- **Eurostat.** [Electricity prices for non-household consumers](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/nrg_pc_205/default/table) and [Labour cost levels by NACE Rev. 2 activity](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/lc_lci_lev/default/table), accessed November 8, 2026. Statistical data reused under [Eurostat's reuse terms](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/help/copyright-notice). Filtered to EU and EFTA reporting countries and normalized by Indexed Drone Industry. Eurostat is not responsible for this adaptation.
- **Natural Earth.** [Natural Earth vector data v5.1.2](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/releases/tag/v5.1.2), 1:110m Admin 0 countries, distributed in `public/geo/countries.geojson`. [Public domain](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/). The map uses these boundaries for display.
