# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and readers with respect. Discuss evidence and behavior on their merits. Welcome questions, credit other contributors, and give people room to correct mistakes.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, and disclosure of private information are prohibited in project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to a project maintainer. Include the relevant link and context, and avoid reposting private details in a public issue. Maintainers review reports discreetly and may edit or remove abusive material, limit participation, or block accounts. If the concern involves a maintainer, contact another maintainer or use GitHub's reporting tools.
